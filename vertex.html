Vertex — Investment Intelligence Platform

Explore Live Website →

Vertex is a quantitative stock intelligence platform designed to identify potential outperformers within the Nifty 100. It uses predictive modeling to estimate the probability of beating the benchmark over the next 63 trading days, along with expected excess returns and the factors influencing each prediction.

250K+ Stock-level daily records
40+ Quantitative factor dimensions
63 Trading-day prediction horizon

The Problem

Identifying stocks that can outperform a market benchmark is more challenging than simply looking at historical returns or individual technical indicators. Investors have access to large amounts of market data, but turning that information into a forward-looking view of potential performance is not straightforward.

I wanted to explore whether predictive modeling could help identify potential Nifty 100 outperformers, estimate their expected excess returns, and make the reasoning behind each prediction understandable.

How I Approached It

I started by defining the prediction objective: estimating whether a stock would outperform the Nifty 100 over the next 63 trading days, rather than predicting its absolute price.

I then developed predictive models using 40+ quantitative factor dimensions across price momentum, trend strength, volume, and downside risk, trained and evaluated on 250K+ stock-level daily records.

The platform brings these predictions together in a dashboard, allowing users to explore potential outperformers, understand the factors influencing each prediction, and evaluate model performance against historical outcomes.

How the Platform Works

Stock Predictions: Evaluates stocks across the Nifty 100 and generates an outperformance probability and expected alpha for each.

Factor Attribution: Breaks down the factors driving each prediction, highlighting bullish signals and potential risks.

Historical Evaluation: Allows users to select historical dates and compare model predictions against actual stock performance.

Product Vision

The next step for Vertex is to transition from historical test data to live market data, enabling it to generate predictions and investment signals based on current market conditions.

The long-term vision for Vertex is to evolve beyond stock-level predictions into an intelligence layer for investment products.

The goal is to build a connected system that supports the investment journey — from discovering opportunities and generating investment signals to constructing and managing portfolios.

Vertex is an experimental quantitative analytics project, not SEBI-registered investment advice or a source of buy/sell recommendations. Historical results do not guarantee future performance.
