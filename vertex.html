Vertex — Investment Intelligence Platform

Explore Live Website

Vertex is a quantitative stock intelligence platform designed to identify potential outperformers within the Nifty 100. It uses predictive modeling to estimate the probability of beating the benchmark over the next 63 trading days, along with expected excess returns and the factors influencing each prediction.

250K+ Stock-level daily records trained & evaluated
40+ Quantitative factor dimensions evaluated
63 Days Trading-day forward prediction horizon

The problem

Identifying stocks that can outperform a market benchmark is more challenging than simply looking at historical returns or individual technical indicators. Investors have access to large amounts of market data, but turning that information into a forward-looking view of potential performance is not straightforward.

I wanted to explore whether predictive modeling could help identify potential Nifty 100 outperformers, estimate their expected excess returns, and make the reasoning behind each prediction understandable.

How I approached it

I started by defining the prediction objective: estimating whether a stock would outperform the Nifty 100 over the next 63 trading days, rather than predicting its absolute price.

I then developed predictive models using 40+ quantitative factor dimensions across price momentum, trend strength, volume, and downside risk, trained and evaluated on 250K+ stock-level daily records.

The product was designed around three outputs — probability of outperformance, expected alpha, and factor attribution — so users could understand not just which stocks the model identified, but also the factors driving each prediction.

How It Works

For each stock, Vertex generates three key outputs:

Outperformance Probability

Likelihood of beating the Nifty 100 index over the next 63 trading days.

Expected Alpha

Estimated excess return generated by the stock relative to the benchmark.

Factor Attribution

Key bullish drivers and downside risks influencing each specific prediction.

Key Features

Prediction Dashboard

View forward stock predictions and shortlist potential outperformers across the Nifty 100 universe.

Explainable Predictions

Explore the quantitative factors and risk attributes contributing to each stock's predicted performance.

Historical Evaluation

Select historical dates and backtest model predictions against actual subsequent stock performance.

Disclaimer: Vertex is an experimental quantitative analytics project, not SEBI-registered investment advice or a source of buy/sell recommendations. Historical results do not guarantee future performance.
