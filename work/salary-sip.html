Product Case Study / Miles Wealth · 2026

Salary SIP

Employee Financial Wellbeing Platform

Product Overview

Salary SIP enables employees to invest a portion of their salary into personalized mutual fund portfolios, with additional contributions from their employers.

My Role

Head of Product — owned product development from ideation to launch, working closely with the founder and development team.

01

Problem & Opportunity

Corporate employees often struggle to build wealth through consistent saving and investing, even with a regular income. Most retail investment products demand continuous manual discipline after monthly expenses have already depleted liquid funds.

Product Opportunity

What if saving happened before spending — directly at the source of income?

02

User Research & Validation

Led 20+ employee and employer interviews to understand financial wellbeing needs, investment habits, and employer benefit expectations.

Employee Insights

  • Employees found it difficult to invest consistently and faced friction when getting started.
  • Employer contributions made the proposition significantly more attractive.
  • Employer involvement helped build trust in the platform and lowered skepticism.

Employer Insights

  • Employers wanted more differentiated, high-retention employee benefits.
  • Financial wellbeing offered an impactful opportunity to add genuine value to talent packages.
  • Contribution costs and administrative friction were the primary adoption concerns.
03

Product Definition & Experience

Defined employee workflows across onboarding, payroll-linked deductions, and employer contributions, translating requirements into product specifications and working with the founder and developers on execution.
