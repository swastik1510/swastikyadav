Salary SIP — Employee Financial Wellbeing Solution

Salary SIP is an employee financial wellbeing solution I led at Miles Wealth. It enables corporate employees to invest a portion of their salary into personalized mutual fund portfolios, with additional contributions from their employers.

Head of Product — Led product strategy, user discovery, MVP definition, product workflows, and ecosystem integrations.

The Problem

Corporate employees may be earning well, yet many remain financially stressed. The problem isn't necessarily low salaries, but how quickly income gets spent before it has a chance to grow through savings and investments. Limited investment knowledge, lack of trust in investment platforms, and fear of market risks further discourage them from investing.

Employers, meanwhile, face challenges with employee engagement and retention. They are looking for differentiated financial wellbeing benefits beyond standard employee benefits, but HR teams are often hesitant to adopt solutions that add payroll administration overhead.

How I Approached It

We started with a fundamental shift in user psychology: what if saving happened before spending, directly at the payroll source? If investments are deducted alongside payroll and supplemented by employer contributions, consistent wealth generation becomes automatic rather than a monthly test of discipline.

I took this hypothesis forward through 20+ qualitative interviews with corporate employees and HR leaders to understand their needs and validate the concept. Employees saw value in employer contributions as an incentive to invest, while employers emphasized the need for minimal administrative overhead.

Based on these insights, I shaped the B2B2C product experience around two key stakeholders — employees and employers — and defined the workflows, core features, and integrations needed to bring the concept to life.
