Salary SIP — Employee Financial Wellbeing Platform

Salary SIP is the employee financial wellbeing platform I owned at Miles Wealth. It enables corporate employees to invest a portion of their salary directly into personalized mutual fund portfolios, with automated matching and additional contributions from their employers.

Head of Product — owned product strategy, user discovery, MVP definition, and ecosystem integrations

The problem

Corporate employees struggle to build wealth through consistent investing, even with a steady monthly income. Most retail investment products demand continuous manual discipline after monthly living expenses have already depleted liquid funds. By the time a salary lands in a savings account, discretionary spending competes directly with savings goals.

Employers, meanwhile, face increasing employee attrition and want high-retention, differentiated financial wellbeing benefits beyond standard group insurance. However, HR teams were resistant to administrative overhead and payroll reconciliation headaches.

How I approached it

I started with a fundamental shift in user psychology: what if saving happened before spending, directly at the payroll source? If investments are deducted alongside payroll and matched by employers, consistent wealth generation becomes automatic rather than a monthly test of discipline.

I conducted 20+ qualitative interviews across corporate employees and HR leaders to validate the model. Employees confirmed that employer matching removed hesitation and established immediate trust, while employers required zero-overhead integration into existing payroll software.

To bring Salary SIP from 0 to 1, I mapped the end-to-end B2B2C user journey and architected the integration rails: connecting Razorpay for employer contribution flows, FundsNet for automated mutual fund transaction execution, and Zoho Payroll to sync employee deduction schedules directly with corporate payroll systems.
