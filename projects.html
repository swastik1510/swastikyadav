Vertex — quantitative stock intelligence platform predicting Nifty 100 outperformance

An ML-powered quantitative stock intelligence platform designed to identify potential outperformers within the Nifty 100. It uses predictive modeling to estimate outperformance probabilities over a 63-day horizon, along with expected alpha and explainable factor attribution.

  • 250K+ stock-level daily records trained & evaluated
  • 40+ quantitative factor dimensions across momentum, volume & risk
  • 63 trading-day forward prediction horizon
  • ~70% classification accuracy on held-out test data
