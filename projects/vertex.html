Vertex — Quantitative Stock Intelligence Platform

View Demo

Vertex is an ML-powered quantitative stock intelligence platform designed to forecast 63-day alpha across the Nifty 100. It combines predictive factor modeling with full attribution transparency, helping investors evaluate benchmark outperformance probabilities and key driving factors.

The problem

Identifying equities capable of beating a market benchmark over a medium-term horizon is challenging. Most retail investors and discretionary analysts rely on lagging historical returns, isolated technical chart patterns, or subjective narratives.

While massive amounts of market data exist, converting price action, volume trends, and risk metrics into a probabilistic, forward-looking view requires rigorous quantitative modeling. Furthermore, most machine learning models in finance function as opaque black boxes, leaving investors without any explanation of why a prediction was made.

How I approached it

I formulated the prediction problem not as forecasting absolute prices, but as estimating 63-trading-day forward excess return (alpha) relative to the Nifty 100 benchmark. This anchored the objective directly around outperforming the market rather than betting on market direction.

I engineered a dataset of over 250,000 stock-level daily observations across 40+ quantitative factor dimensions, spanning short- and medium-term price momentum, trend stability, volume shocks, and downside volatility risk.

The platform was designed around three clear, explainable outputs: outperformance probability, expected alpha, and factor attribution. By highlighting the primary bullish drivers and risk factors for each stock, investors can see the exact quantitative rationale behind every forecast.

Disclaimer: Vertex is an experimental quantitative analytics project built for demonstration and research, not SEBI-registered investment advice or a solicitation for securities.
